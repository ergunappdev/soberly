# Support

We're here to help with questions, feedback, or issues related to Soberly.

## Getting Help

If you experience a problem with the app, have a question about a feature, or would like to share feedback, please contact us at:

**[ergun.app.dev@gmail.com](mailto:ergun.app.dev@gmail.com)**

When contacting support, including the following information can help us assist you more quickly:

* A description of the issue
* The steps that caused the problem
* Your device model
* Your iOS version
* Your Soberly app version
* Screenshots, if applicable

Please do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

## Subscriptions and Purchases

If you have a question about a subscription or purchase, please contact us at:

**[ergun.app.dev@gmail.com](mailto:ergun.app.dev@gmail.com)**

Purchases made through the App Store are processed by Apple. Soberly does not have access to your payment card or banking information.

For billing, refund, or payment-related issues, you may also need to contact Apple directly.

## Feedback

We welcome your feedback and suggestions. If there is a feature you'd like to see in Soberly or you have an idea for improving the app, feel free to contact us.

## Privacy Requests

If you have questions about how Soberly handles your information or would like to request deletion of information associated with your account, contact us at:

**[ergun.app.dev@gmail.com](mailto:ergun.app.dev@gmail.com)**

Please include enough information for us to understand and process your request.

## Technical Issues

If Soberly is crashing, not displaying your progress correctly, or a feature is not working as expected, please contact us with:

* A description of the problem
* What you were doing when the problem occurred
* Your device model
* Your iOS version
* Your Soberly app version
* Screenshots or screen recordings, if available

This information helps us investigate and resolve technical issues more efficiently.

## Important

Soberly is a wellness and tracking application. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency or require professional assistance, please seek help from a qualified healthcare professional or appropriate emergency service.

## Contact

For support, questions, feedback, technical issues, subscription questions, or privacy-related requests:

**[ergun.app.dev@gmail.com](mailto:ergun.app.dev@gmail.com)**

We aim to review support requests and respond as soon as reasonably possible.
