# Disclaimer

Soberly is a personal tracking and wellness application designed to help users monitor and stay motivated throughout their alcohol-free journey.

## Not Medical Advice

Soberly does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or professional healthcare services. The information and features provided by the app are for general informational and self-tracking purposes only.

Soberly should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Alcohol Withdrawal

Stopping or significantly reducing alcohol consumption can be medically dangerous for some people. Alcohol withdrawal may cause serious or life-threatening symptoms.

If you regularly consume alcohol heavily or believe you may experience withdrawal symptoms, consult a qualified healthcare professional before stopping alcohol use.

If you experience severe or emergency symptoms, seek immediate medical attention.

## Personal Responsibility

Soberly does not guarantee any particular outcome from using the app. Users are responsible for their own decisions regarding alcohol consumption and their health.

Any statistics, estimates, milestones, savings calculations, or progress information displayed by Soberly are intended for motivational and informational purposes and may not represent exact medical or financial measurements.

## Emergency Situations

Soberly is not an emergency service and should not be relied upon during a medical or mental health emergency.

If you believe you are experiencing an emergency, contact your local emergency services or seek immediate professional assistance.
