# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 24, 2026**

Soberly ("Soberly", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Soberly application.

## 1. Information We Collect

Soberly may process the following information depending on the features you use:

### Information You Provide

If you use Sign in with Apple, we may receive information provided by Apple, such as your name and email address, depending on the options you select.

### Sober Progress Data

Information you enter into Soberly, such as your sobriety start date, drinking-related goals, milestones, and progress information, may be stored locally on your device to provide the app's features.

### Subscription Information

If you purchase a subscription or other premium feature, payments are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card or banking information.

Subscription status may be processed through our subscription management service to provide access to premium features.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by Soberly may be used to:

* Provide and maintain app functionality
* Calculate sobriety duration and progress
* Display milestones and statistics
* Provide widgets and other requested features
* Manage premium features and subscriptions
* Respond to support requests
* Improve the reliability and functionality of the app

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Data Storage

Soberly is designed to keep your personal progress information private. Data that is stored locally remains on your device unless you explicitly use a feature that requires information to be transmitted to a third-party service.

## 4. Sign in with Apple

If you choose to use Sign in with Apple, authentication is provided by Apple.

Apple may provide Soberly with information associated with your Apple account according to the choices you make during sign-in. You may also choose to hide your personal email address by using Apple's private relay email feature.

For more information about Apple's privacy practices, please refer to Apple's privacy policy.

## 5. Payments and Subscriptions

Purchases made through Soberly are processed by Apple through the App Store.

Soberly does not collect or store your credit card, debit card, or other payment information.

Subscription information may be processed by our subscription management provider solely to verify purchases and provide access to premium features.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Soberly may use third-party services necessary to provide specific functionality, such as authentication and subscription management.

These services may process limited information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

## 7. Notifications and Widgets

If you enable notifications, Soberly may use information stored on your device to schedule and display reminders or progress notifications.

Widget information is generated from data available to the app and may be displayed on your device according to your iOS settings.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by Soberly can generally be removed by deleting the application or using available deletion options within the app.

If you use an account or a service that stores information externally, you may contact us to request deletion of applicable personal information.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Soberly is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Soberly. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

## 12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Soberly handles information, please contact us through the support information provided in the app.
