# About Soberly

Soberly is a simple and supportive app designed to help you track your alcohol-free journey and stay motivated along the way.

With Soberly, you can track your sober days, monitor your progress, see the time and money you've saved, and reflect on the positive changes you're making.

## Our Goal

Soberly was created to make the journey toward drinking less or staying alcohol-free easier to understand and more motivating.

Whether you're taking your first step, starting again, or maintaining your progress, Soberly gives you a clear view of how far you've come.

## Features

* Track your alcohol-free days
* Monitor your progress and milestones
* Track estimated money saved
* View time saved from drinking
* Celebrate personal milestones
* Use widgets for quick progress updates
* Access simple and motivating statistics

## Important Note

Soberly is a tracking and wellness tool. It is not a medical service and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

If you need professional help with alcohol use, please contact a qualified healthcare professional or appropriate support service.
